Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled by the Fenix 8 · Pro Max Data watch face developed for Garmin devices using the Garmin Connect IQ platform.

1. General Information

The application does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. All data accessed by the application remains on the user’s Garmin device and within the Garmin Connect ecosystem.

2. Data Accessed by the Application

The application may access the following data locally on the device, only for display purposes on the watch face:

  • Time and date
  • Battery level
  • Step count
  • Heart rate
  • Altitude
  • Weather information (temperature and conditions, if enabled)
  • Other activity-related metrics selected by the user

3. Data Storage

The application does not permanently store any personal data. Any data used is processed temporarily in device memory and is not persisted.

4. Data Sharing

The application does not share data with third parties, does not transmit data over the internet, and does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

5. User Control

Users have full control over permissions granted through Garmin Connect and can modify or revoke access at any time via Garmin settings.

6. Legal Basis (GDPR)

Data processing is based on user consent, as defined in Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Children’s Privacy

The application is not intended for use by children under the age of 16 and does not knowingly collect any personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of the application changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.
